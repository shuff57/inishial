Your grade

Scale: A 90+ · B 80+ · C 70+ · D 60+

Turning work in late

  • Late passes: 10 per semester, one extra day each
  • Use a pass the day you miss the due date, they do not stack
  • Passes work on Daily Homework/Classwork only, never on assessments
  • Working ahead: finish more than twenty-four hours early and shCode adds 2% automatically

When you are absent

  • Excused: the same number of days you were out, counted from the day you return
  • Unexcused: no extension, the work is still due on its original date
  • Planned ahead: arrange it before you leave, or a missed assessment stays missed

Assessments

  • No retakes on Group or Individual Assessments, and no corrections
  • Graded within 48 hours, daily work is scored immediately in shCode
  • Individual Assessments are quizzes, chapter tests, the four synthesis projects, and the two semester finals

In the room

  • Phones: collected at the start of every period, returned at the end
  • Earbuds, smart watches and smart glasses go with them
  • Bring: charged Chromebook, notebook for design sketches and pseudocode
  • AI: unauthorized use is cheating, edited or not

For parents

  • This is Butte College CSCI 4, 3 units, on a permanent college transcript
  • Butte receives one grade for the year, the average of both semester percentages
  • Nothing to buy or install, the work runs in the browser in shCode
  • Check grades: shCode, for anything missing, late or open
