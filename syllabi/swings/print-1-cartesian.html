Turning work in late

  • Late passes: ten per semester, one extra day each.
  • Use a pass the day you miss the due date. They do not stack.

When you are absent

  • Excused: the same number of days you were out, counted from the day you return.
  • Unexcused: no extension. Still due on its original date.
  • Planned ahead: arrange it before you leave, or a missed test stays missed.
