Article II

Late work

Excused absence
The same number of days you were out, counted from the day you come back. Absent two days, you have two.
Unexcused absence
Extends nothing. The work is still due on its original date.
An absence you saw coming
Arrange it before you leave. Say nothing until you are back and the work falls under this policy, and a missed test stays missed.
Late passes
Ten per semester. One pass, one assignment, one extra day. Use it the day you miss the deadline. They do not stack and they do not apply to tests.
Group and Individual Tests
No retakes, no corrections An excused absence gives you time to take the test you missed, not a second attempt at one you took.
Practice Tests
The exception. Redo them as many times as you want. If you cannot do one without help, you are not ready for the test.
Article III

Finish an assignment more than twenty-four hours before it is due and MyOpenMath adds 2% to it automatically. You do not have to ask.

For parents and guardians

Why this one counts more than a high school class

It is permanent

The grade becomes part of a Butte College transcript and follows your student to any college they transfer to. Four units, at no cost.

Both halves matter

Butte averages the two semester percentages equally. A rough fall is recoverable. A strong fall is not a reason to coast.
