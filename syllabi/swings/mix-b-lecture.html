03Late work

  • Late passes: ten per semester, one extra day each.
  • Use a pass the day you miss the due date. They do not stack.
  • Daily classwork and homework only, never on tests.
  • Working ahead: finish more than twenty-four hours early and MyOpenMath adds 2% automatically.

04Absence

  • Excused: the same number of days you were out, counted from the day you return.
  • Unexcused: no extension. Still due on its original date.
  • Planned ahead: arrange it before you leave, or a missed test stays missed.

05Assessments

  • No retakes on Group or Individual Tests, and no corrections.
  • Practice Tests are homework. Redo them as many times as you want.
  • Cannot do one without help? You are not ready.

06In the room

  • Phones collected at the start of every period, returned at the end.
  • Earbuds, smart watches and smart glasses go with them.
  • Bring: charged Chromebook, notebook with lined and graph paper.
  • AI: unauthorized use is cheating, edited or not.

07For parents

  • Four units on a permanent college transcript.
  • Butte receives one grade for the year: the average of both semester percentages.
  • Flat letters at Butte, so 89.9% is a B there, not a B+.
  • Check MyOpenMath for anything missing, late or open.
