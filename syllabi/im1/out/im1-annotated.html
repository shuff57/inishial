Contact

  • Email: shuff@chicousd.org, I reply within 48 hours
  • No reply by then: send a follow-up
  • Current grade: MyOpenMath, more current than Aeries check here first
  • Official grade: Aeries, reported by semester

Your grade

Scale: A 90+ · B 80+ · C 70+ · D 60+

Turning work in late

  • Late passes: 10 per semester, one extra day each
  • Use a pass the day you miss the due date, they do not stack
  • Passes work on daily classwork and homework only, never on tests
  • Working ahead: finish more than twenty-four hours early and MyOpenMath adds 2% automatically

When you are absent

  • Excused: the same number of days you were out, counted from the day you return
  • Unexcused: no extension, the work is still due on its original date
  • Planned ahead: arrange it before you leave, or a missed test stays missed

Tests

  • No retakes on Group Tests or Individual Tests, and no corrections
  • Practice Tests: redo as many times as you want, they are homework
  • Cannot do the Practice Test without help? You are not ready

In the room

  • Phones: collected at the start of every period, returned at the end
  • Earbuds, smart watches and smart glasses go with them
  • Bring: charged Chromebook, notebook with lined and graph paper
  • AI: unauthorized use is cheating, edited or not

For parents

  • Nothing to buy, the book is free at oerbookshelf.app and downloads for offline use
  • Two semester grades, December and June, both on the transcript, neither replaces the other
  • Graded work lives in MyOpenMath, which needs internet, so download ahead if you will be offline
  • Check grades: MyOpenMath, for anything missing, late or open
